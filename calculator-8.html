Isolated margin is assumed. The liquidation price is an estimate based on Hyperliquid's formula (maintenance margin is taken from the coin's max leverage), so the real number can differ slightly. The fee is charged twice: once when you open the trade (entry) and once when you close it (exit). There are two fee boxes: the entry fee is charged on the order that opens the trade (usually a market order, taker) and the exit fee on the order that closes it (usually a limit order, maker). Defaults for crypto are 0.045% entry + 0.015% exit (0.06% total), and 0.0675% + 0.0225% (0.09% total) for non-crypto markets. The stop loss and target exits both use the exit fee box. Change the boxes if your fees differ. Funding fees are not included. This is only a calculator, not financial advice.
