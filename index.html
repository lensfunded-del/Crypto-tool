Isolated margin is assumed. The liquidation price is an estimate based on Hyperliquid's formula (maintenance margin is taken from the coin's max leverage), so the real number can differ slightly. The fee is charged twice: once when you open the trade (entry) and once when you close it (exit). The default is 0.045% taker per side, so a full round trip costs about 0.09%. The leverage slider goes up to each coin's own max leverage on Hyperliquid (for example BTC is higher than small coins). Very large positions can have a lower max leverage than shown. Change it to match your tier or prop firm. Funding fees are not included. This is only a calculator, not financial advice.
